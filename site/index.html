simbiguity

An open source simulation engine by The Ambiguity Project

Demos

  • Hello, circuit: close a switch and light a bulb, with live voltage, current and power from the circuits engine running as WebAssembly.
  • Circuit in 3D: the same circuit on a board you can orbit, with charge flowing along the wires as fast as the solved current and a bulb that warms up and cools down.

More are on the way, starting with a 3D circuit puzzle.
