Circuit in 3D

The same battery, switch and 12 V / 6 W bulb as Hello, circuit, now on a board you can turn around. The glowing dots are charge: they move as fast as the solved current says, and the bulb warms up as bright as the power it absorbs. Rust builds every frame; WebGL2 only draws it.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click the switch to flip it.
